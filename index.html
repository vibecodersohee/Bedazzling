Gesture guide

Your hand lights up the matching card. Try them! (Mouse works too.)

👋Swipe

Open flat hand, sweep left or right to change color. (Arrow keys on keyboard.)

🎨Tools

Hold over or pinch a tool on the left (Gem, Brush, Erase, Clear) and a size on the right.

🤏Pinch

Thumb + index together over a gem, drag onto your face, open to drop. Also presses buttons (or just hold over a button for a second).

☝️Point

Index finger only. With the Brush tool, gems trail along your fingertip.

✊Fist

Hold half a second to undo.

👍Thumbs up

Hold one second to finish and take your selfie.

Tip: pinch a gem on your face and drag it off to remove it. Mouse: drag to place, right-click removes, Z undoes, Enter finishes.
